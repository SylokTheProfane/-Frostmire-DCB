PERSONNEL FILE PSYCHOLOGICAL RELATIONS

Psychological Overview

Elise Belmont presents as a rather reserved person, outside of personnel she trusts she often remains silent. Often being uncomfortable with large groups of people during normal interaction, yet shows comfort when with the same amount of people during a stressful situation.

Despite this reserved nature, Elise has repeatedly demonstrated a willingness to employ violence when she considers it necessary. This tendency is most likely genetic as her father exhibited the same tendencies.

Elise is frequently approached by personnel seeking someone willing to listen without immediately documentation or judgement unlike standard procedure for Foundation Medical Staff. Several individuals have informally described this behavior as "therapist-like". Yet when Elise is put into medical situations, she is often uncomfortable and has said quote "Rather have my teeth pulled without anesthetic than have medical touch me"

Documented Conditions

PTSD

Elise experiences frequent PTSD associated with the death of her mother, due to a motor vehicle accident as a child. Often presenting during Nightmares or when tires start to skid. When triggered, she often hyperfocuses on a singular item in her hand.

Should this trigger at an inconvient time, it could prove dangerous to her, or even fatal. Elise should be monitored for if this behavior response changes. Should it change It is to be documented immediatly and she is to be given a psychological evaulation.

INSOMNIA

Elise has inconsistent sleep patterns, having these issues for a majority of her adult life. She has been previously medicated for it, yet found little success.

The fatigue from Insomnia has leaked into her other nighttime operations, leading to her attention to detail fading as the day passes. Often choosing to constantly drink energy drinks and has a canteen of it on her person daily.

Behavioral Assessment

Elise's history has not resulted in an observable abandonment of responsibility or interpersonal loyalty. Instead, she appears to maintain a strong tendency toward protecting individuals who have earned her trust.

Her willingness to function as a support pillar for others is one of the more consistent characteristics documented in her personnel record.

This behavior is particularly notable given her own history of isolation following the death of her mother and subsequent estrangement from her father.

Elise's military background and later work as a contractor appear to have reinforced an independent approach to problem solving.

Operational Skillset

Elise demonstrates a mixed skillset. She possesses long-range recon skills associated with her mother's background while also displaying a willingness to engage targets at close range thanks to her father.

Elise uses her "Jack Of All Trades" skills to be a versatile operator, yet as comes with the tradeoff, she has not mastered any skills. Pairing her with other Operatives who are more skilled than her may prove to her benefit.

Psychological Factors

Following the death of Klara Frenken, Rios Belmont reportedly distanced himself from Elise after recovering from the accident. After the seperation, Elise made it her goal to find her father and reunite.

Her subsequent decision to leave high school and enter military service mirrored the career path of her father, this could be seen as a possible flaw for being too attached towards family.

"Whatever happened between them, she never stopped looking for him."
