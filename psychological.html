PERSONNEL FILE PSYCHOLOGICAL RELATIONS

Psychological Overview

Elise Belmont presents as a straightforward individual with a generally restrained interpersonal style. She is not particularly inclined toward unnecessary conversation and has demonstrated a preference for direct communication.

Despite this reserved nature, Belmont has repeatedly demonstrated a willingness to employ violence when she considers it necessary. Personnel reports indicate that this tendency does not appear to prevent her from maintaining close interpersonal relationships.

Belmont is frequently approached by personnel seeking someone willing to listen without immediately judging or attempting to intervene. Several individuals have informally described this behavior as "therapist-like."

"She's quiet, like the feeling of the ocean waves."

Documented Conditions

PTSD

Belmont experiences PTSD associated with the death of her mother, Klara Frenken, in a motor vehicle accident.

The incident occurred during her childhood and remains a significant component of her psychological history.

INSOMNIA

Subject reports persistent difficulty maintaining regular sleep patterns.

Resulting fatigue has been documented during nighttime operations and other extended periods of activity.

Behavioral Assessment

Belmont's history has not resulted in an observable abandonment of responsibility or interpersonal loyalty. Instead, she appears to maintain a strong tendency toward protecting individuals who have earned her trust.

Her willingness to function as a support pillar for others is one of the more consistent characteristics documented in her personnel record.

This behavior is particularly notable given her own history of isolation following the death of her mother and subsequent estrangement from her father.

Belmont's military background and later work as a contractor appear to have reinforced an independent approach to problem solving.

Operational Psychology

Subject demonstrates a mixed operational profile. She possesses long-range reconnaissance skills associated with her mother's background while also displaying a willingness to engage targets at close range.

This produces a flexible but broad skill set rather than a single highly specialized operational discipline.

Frostmire's tendency to protect individuals she considers part of her circle should be considered when assigning personnel to prolonged operations.

Historical Psychological Factors

Following the death of Klara Frenken, Rios Belmont reportedly distanced himself from Elise after recovering from the accident. Elise interpreted the resulting separation as a significant development in her life.

Her subsequent decision to leave high school and enter military service mirrored the career path of her father.

After leaving the military, she began searching independently for information concerning Rios. This investigation ultimately contributed to her discovery near the Veil in Alaska.

"Whatever happened between them, she never stopped looking for him."
